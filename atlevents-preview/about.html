About ATL Events

Events planned with intention, taste, and steady hands.

Our approach

We listen first, then build the plan around the people in the room.

All Things Love Events creates weddings and celebrations that reflect each client’s vision, culture, family, and priorities. The work is detailed, but the experience should feel calm.

  • Client satisfaction: clear communication, realistic planning, and thoughtful decisions from inquiry to event day.
  • Attention to detail: the timeline, vendor handoffs, design choices, and guest movement all matter.
  • Less stress for the client: the planning team carries the moving pieces so the event can be experienced, not managed.
