Quick answers

Questions people ask before they book.

What does an Atlanta wedding planner do?

A wedding planner helps organize budgets, vendors, design direction, timelines, logistics, guest experience, family details, and wedding-day execution.

Do we need full planning or partial planning?

Full planning is best early in the process. Partial planning is better when vendors or major decisions are already started but the plan needs structure and professional review.

Does ATL Events work outside Atlanta?

ATL Events serves Atlanta, Georgia, and destination celebrations when the event scope is the right fit.
