Wedding coordination

When the plans are made, the day still needs a leader.

ATL Events turns vendor details, ceremony cues, room flips, family needs, and timing into a wedding day that moves with calm precision.

Coordination scope

Built for couples who need professional execution, not guesswork.

Coordination should start before the wedding weekend. The strongest version reviews vendor contracts, builds the timeline, identifies missing details, leads rehearsal flow, and manages the event day.

  • Timeline creation and final planning review.
  • Vendor confirmation and communication.
  • Rehearsal and ceremony processional support.
  • Event-day logistics, setup checks, guest flow, and problem solving.

Quick answers

Questions people ask before they book.

Is day-of coordination really only one day?

No. Strong coordination starts before the wedding day so the timeline, vendors, rehearsal, ceremony flow, setup, and logistics can be prepared.

What does a wedding coordinator manage?

A coordinator manages the timeline, vendor communication, rehearsal flow, setup checks, ceremony cues, guest movement, and event-day problem solving.

Who is coordination best for?

Coordination is best for couples who planned most details but want a professional to organize the final handoff and lead the wedding day.
