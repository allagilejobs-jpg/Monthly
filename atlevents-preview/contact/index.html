Quick answers

Questions people ask before they book.

What should we include in an inquiry?

Share the event date, location, guest count, planning stage, service needs, and any design or cultural priorities that matter most.

How quickly should we reach out?

Reach out as soon as you know the event date or planning support needed, especially for weddings and high-demand seasons.

Can we ask which service is the best fit?

Yes. The consultation can help identify whether full planning, partial planning, coordination, design, or consultation is the right next step.
