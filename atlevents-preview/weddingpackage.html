Quick answers

Questions people ask before they book.

Which wedding package should we choose?

Full planning fits couples who want guidance from the beginning, partial planning fits couples who already started, and coordination fits couples who need professional timeline and event-day leadership.

When should we book wedding coordination?

Coordination works best when it starts before the final month, so vendor contracts, timelines, ceremony flow, and missing details can be reviewed properly.

Can packages be adjusted for multicultural weddings?

Yes. Planning support can account for family logistics, traditions, multiple ceremonies, guest movement, and culturally specific details.
