Planning questions

Helpful answers for couples comparing Atlanta wedding planners.

  • When should we hire a wedding planner?
    For full planning, start as early as possible. For coordination, start before the final month so the timeline and vendor details can be reviewed properly.
  • Do you plan multicultural weddings?
    Yes. ATL Events supports celebrations with family traditions, cultural details, multiple events, and guest experience planning.
  • What should we share before a consultation?
    Share the event date, guest count, location, planning stage, service needs, and the traditions or design priorities that matter most.
  • Do you handle corporate and private events?
    Yes. The planning process can support private celebrations, brand moments, dinners, and corporate gatherings.
