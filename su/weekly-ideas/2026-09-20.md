## 1. Educational

**Post type:** Carousel

**Hook/headline:** When the school app says "missing assignment" again

**Key points/script outline:**
- Slide 1: When school communication becomes a second job
- Slide 2: Autistic kids may not reliably report homework, deadlines, schedule changes, or social stress
- Slide 3: Ask school for one source of truth, not five scattered apps
- Slide 4: Create a weekly 10-minute check-in with teacher, parent, and child supports
- Slide 5: Use a "what changed today?" script after school instead of broad questions
- Slide 6: Save screenshots of key messages for IEP/504 documentation
- Slide 7: Parent reminder: overwhelmed by admin does not mean you are failing

**Suggested hashtags:** #autismparenting #autismsupport #specialneedsparenting #iepteam #504plan #backtoschool #neurodivergentkids #parentingtips

## 2. Educational

**Post type:** Reel

**Hook/headline:** Try the 3-2-1 after-school reset

**Key points/script outline:**
- Opening: "If your child falls apart after school, try this before asking questions."
- 3 minutes: no demands, no questions, quiet transition
- 2 choices: snack choice, movement choice, or quiet activity choice
- 1 connection cue: "I'm glad you're home" or a preferred greeting
- Close: "Regulation first. Processing later."

**Suggested hashtags:** #autismafterchool #afterSchoolRestraintCollapse #autismmom #autismdad #sensoryneeds #emotionalregulation #neurodiversityaffirming
