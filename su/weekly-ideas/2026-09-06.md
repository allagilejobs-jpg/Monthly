**Script outline:**
- 0-3 sec: "Try this when your child seems off but cannot explain why."
- 3-10 sec: Show simple visual choices: hungry, bathroom, tired, too loud, hot, worried
- 10-18 sec: "Many autistic kids feel body signals late or differently."

- 30-42 sec: Examples: Body means food, bathroom, sleep. Brain means worry or too much thinking. Space means noise, lights, people, or demands.
- 42-50 sec: "You are not forcing words. You are giving them a map."
