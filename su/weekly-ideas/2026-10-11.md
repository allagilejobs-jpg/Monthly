## 2. Educational

**Post type:** Reel

**Hook/headline:** When your child scripts lines from shows, try this response

**Key points/script outline:**
- Opening: "If your child repeats lines from shows or videos, it may be communication, not nonsense."
- Beat 1: Pause and look for the feeling or memory behind the script.
- Beat 2: Reply to the meaning you suspect: "That sounds like you're worried," or "You want to leave?"
- Beat 3: Offer a bridge phrase: "You can say, 'I need a break.'"
- Beat 4: Accept the script while adding context, instead of demanding a different sentence.
- Close: "Scripting can be language with a doorway. Look for the doorway."

**Suggested hashtags:** #GestaltLanguageProcessing #AutismCommunication #AutismParenting #SpeechSupport #NeurodivergentKids #BehaviorIsCommunication #AutismTips #SpectrumUnlocked
