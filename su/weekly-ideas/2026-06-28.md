## 1. Educational: Elopement Is Not “Running Away”

**Post type:** Carousel  
**Hook/headline:** “If your autistic child bolts, it may be communication before it is behavior.”

**Key points:**
1. Slide 1: Hook, “Elopement is not just running away.”
2. Slide 2: Define elopement/wandering in parent-friendly language.
3. Slide 3: Common reasons: escape noise, seek movement, chase interest, avoid demand, find water, panic response.

## 2. Educational: Food Jags Without The Panic

**Post type:** Reel  
**Hook/headline:** “If your child only eats the same few foods, you are not failing. Their nervous system may be choosing safety.”

**Script outline:**
- “A food jag is when a child wants the same foods over and over, then sometimes suddenly drops one.”
- “For autistic kids, sameness can mean safety: same texture, same smell, same temperature, same packaging.”
- “Pressure often backfires because it makes food feel less safe.”
- “Try food chaining: change one tiny thing at a time, like shape, brand, dip, plate, or temperature.”
- “Keep safe foods available while gently building tolerance around new foods.”
- “If intake is very limited, weight drops, choking happens, or mealtimes feel traumatic, ask your pediatrician about feeding therapy.”
- “Fed and safe comes before variety.”

**Suggested hashtags:**  
#AutismAndFood #PickyEating #FoodJags #AutismParenting #SensoryProcessing #FeedingTherapy #NeurodivergentKids #SpectrumUnlocked
