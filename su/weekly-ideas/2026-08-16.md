## 1. Educational

**Post type:** Carousel

**Hook/headline:** "If your child refuses group work, check the hidden social load."

**Key points or script outline:**
1. Slide 1: Hook, "Group work can be harder than the worksheet."
2. Slide 2: Autistic kids may be managing noise, turn-taking, unclear roles, peer judgment, and fast instructions at the same time.
3. Slide 3: What parents may hear: "I hate school," "Nobody likes me," "The work is stupid," or silence.
4. Slide 4: What teachers may see: refusal, shutdown, controlling the task, walking away, or correcting peers.
5. Slide 5: Support idea: give a clear role before the group starts.
6. Slide 6: Support idea: allow a paired partner, written steps, or a quiet contribution option.
7. Slide 7: Parent script: "Can we reduce the social guessing in group assignments?"
8. Slide 8: CTA: "Save this before the next school email."

## 2. Educational

**Post type:** Reel

**Hook/headline:** "The substitute teacher plan your autistic child needs before the sub walks in."

**Key points or script outline:**
- Open with text overlay: "A substitute can change the whole nervous system."
- Script beat 1: "For some autistic kids, a substitute teacher is not a small change. It means a new voice, new rules, new pacing, and less predictability."
- Script beat 2: "Ask the school to keep a simple sub note: communication style, sensory supports, bathroom or break rules, preferred calm-down strategy, and one thing not to do."
- Script beat 3: "If your child uses visuals, the sub needs access to them too."
- Script beat 4: "The goal is not special treatment. The goal is continuity."
- CTA: "Comment SUB and we will share a quick template."
