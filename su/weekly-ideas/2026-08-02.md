## 1. Educational

**Post type:** Carousel  
**Hook/headline:** "Back-to-school clothing battles may be sensory data, not stubbornness."

**Key points/script outline:**
1. Slide 1: Hook, "Before the first school morning fight, check the clothes."
2. Slide 2: New shoes, collars, seams, socks, waistbands, uniforms, and tags can all stack sensory stress.
3. Slide 3: A child may not know how to say, "This hurts," so it comes out as refusing, crying, hiding, or yelling.
4. Slide 4: Test outfits before school starts, not on the morning you need to leave.
5. Slide 5: Make a yes/no clothing basket: safe socks, backup pants, tagless shirts, soft layers, preferred shoes.
6. Slide 6: Take photos of approved outfits so mornings have fewer decisions.
7. Slide 7: If uniforms are required, ask about sensory-friendly accommodations early.
8. Slide 8: CTA: "Save this before the first school morning."

## 2. Educational

**Post type:** Reel  
**Hook/headline:** "The first school week is not the week to add new demands."

**Key points/script outline:**
- "Back-to-school already asks a lot from an autistic nervous system."
- "New room, new smells, new sounds, new adults, new rules, new transitions."
- "This week, lower demands at home where you can."
- "Keep dinners simple. Reduce extra errands. Delay non-urgent chores. Let safe routines be safe."
- "After school, try snack, quiet, movement, or screen decompression before questions."
- "You are not lowering the bar. You are lowering the load."
