## 2. Educational

**Post type:** Reel

**Hook/headline:** Try this instead of "use your words"

**Key points/script outline:**
- Opening: "If your child freezes, screams, runs, or repeats the same phrase, 'use your words' may be too much."
- Beat 1: Offer two choices: "Point or show me."
- Beat 2: Model the sentence without requiring a repeat: "You can say, 'too loud.'"
- Beat 3: Use visuals or objects: snack, break, help, hurt, bathroom, all done.
- Beat 4: Praise communication in any form, including pointing, AAC, gesture, scripting, or bringing you the item.
- Close: "Communication is the goal. Speech is one possible route."

**Suggested hashtags:** #AutismCommunication #AACAwareness #AutismTips #NeurodivergentKids #BehaviorIsCommunication #AutismParenting #SpeechSupport #SpectrumUnlocked
