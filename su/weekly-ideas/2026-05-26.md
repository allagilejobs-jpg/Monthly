## 1. Educational: The Summer Routine Reset

**Post type:** Carousel  
**Hook/headline:** “School is ending. Your child may need a new rhythm before they need new rules.”

**Key points:**
1. Slide 1: Hook, “Summer breaks routines, not just schedules.”
2. Slide 2: Why autistic kids can struggle when school structure disappears: predictability, transitions, sensory load, sleep shifts.
3. Slide 3: Pick 3 anchors for the day: wake-up rhythm, meal rhythm, bedtime rhythm.
4. Slide 4: Use a “today board” instead of a full weekly schedule if the week keeps changing.
5. Slide 5: Preview changes in tiny chunks: “First breakfast, then library, then home.”
6. Slide 6: Build in recovery time after outings, even fun outings.
7. Slide 7: CTA: “Comment SUMMER if you want a simple visual routine starter.”

## 2. Educational: Why “Fun” Can Still Cause Meltdowns

**Post type:** Reel  
**Hook/headline:** “A birthday party, splash pad, or family cookout can be fun and overwhelming at the same time.”

**Script outline:**
- “Autism parenting reminder: fun does not cancel out sensory overload.”
- “Your child can love the pool and still crash afterward.”
- “They can enjoy family and still need space from noise, touch, smells, heat, and surprise attention.”
- “Look for the early signs: faster stimming, hiding, asking to leave, getting silly, getting bossy, repeating questions, or suddenly refusing everything.”
- “Try a quiet exit plan before you arrive: where can they go, who takes them, what do they bring?”
- “The goal is not to avoid every hard moment. The goal is to support their nervous system before it hits emergency mode.”

**Suggested hashtags:**  
#SensoryOverload #AutismParenting #AutisticChildren #MeltdownSupport #SensoryProcessing #NeurodivergentKids #AutismTips #SpectrumUnlocked

## 3. Emotional/Relatable: The Parent Who Leaves Early

**Post type:** Single quote graphic  
**Hook/headline:** “If you left early to protect your child’s peace, you did not fail.”

**Key points/caption angle:**
- Validate the parent who leaves the cookout, church event, birthday party, grocery store, or family gathering early.
- Name the guilt: feeling rude, judged, dramatic, or like everyone thinks you are “giving in.”
- Reframe leaving as attunement and prevention, not failure.
- Caption line: “Sometimes the most loving parenting decision is the one nobody else understands.”

## 6. Engagement: Finish The Sentence

**Post type:** Carousel or story

**Key points:**
