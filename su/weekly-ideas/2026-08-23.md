## 2. Educational

**Post type:** Reel  
**Hook/headline:** "When your child says 'I do not know,' they may not be avoiding the question."

**Key points/script outline:**
- Open with text overlay: "'I don't know' can be a nervous system answer."
- Beat 1: "Some autistic kids need more processing time, especially after a noisy or demanding day."
- Beat 2: "They may know what happened but not have the words yet."
- Beat 3: "Try choices instead of open-ended questions: 'Was it loud, confusing, boring, or okay?'"
- Beat 4: "Try later, after food, movement, quiet, or a favorite activity."
- Beat 5: "Communication support is not letting them avoid. It is helping their brain access the answer."
- CTA: "Comment WORDS if after-school questions are hard in your house."
