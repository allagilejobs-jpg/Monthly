## Notes for Voice and Safety

- Avoid inspiration-porn framing during Disability Pride Month.
- Do not make parents the hero of disability pride. Center the child's dignity, access, and shame-free belonging.
- Use identity-first and person-first language flexibly, but avoid puzzle-piece/cure language.
- For stimming, only discuss redirecting when there is safety risk. Otherwise normalize and support.
- For water safety and elopement, stay calm, practical, and non-fearmongering.
- Captions and graphics: no em dashes. Use commas, colons, hyphens, or line breaks.
