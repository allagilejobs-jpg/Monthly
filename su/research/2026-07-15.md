# Spectrum Unlocked Content Research
**Date:** 2026-07-15 (Wednesday)
**Focus:** Autism parenting niche trends, awareness dates, content gaps, and fresh evergreen angles

## 1) What is trending this week

### Top trending topic (this week): Back-to-school autism transition prep starts now
Strong current signal across autism parenting content is early school-transition planning in mid-July, not late August. Repeated themes: routine reset, school walkthroughs, IEP/504 readiness, teacher communication, and sensory prep kits.

Why this is hot now:
- Families are in planning mode in July
- Providers are publishing practical checklists now
- This topic drives saves and shares because it is tactical and time-sensitive

### Secondary trend: Disability Pride Month + dignity-first parenting
July Disability Pride content is active, especially around inclusion, accommodations, and everyday dignity in public/school spaces.

### Third trend: Practical sensory shopping content
Autism/ADHD sensory-tool shopping roundups are getting seasonal traction (summer sales and school prep), especially when framed as "what actually helps regulation".

## 3) Content gaps (topics competitors/market are pushing that SU has not fully shipped)

Context checked: SU content hub currently shows **15 days ready**, with strong base coverage (myths, acceptance, IEP basics, sensory basics, burnout, meltdowns, feeding, sleep, etc.).

### Gap A: July-to-August school transition playbooks
Competitor content is very tactical right now with step-by-step back-to-school checklists.
- Opportunity: create SU-specific, printable "7-day school launch" assets
- Format: carousel + downloadable checklist + short script reel

### Gap B: ADA rights translated for parents in plain language
Current market content often mentions accommodations but not parent-ready scripts tied to rights.
- Opportunity: "What to say in school meetings" rights-to-action mini series

### Gap C: Sensory regulation kits for classroom, bus, and after-school crash
Seasonal shopping and prep content is rising, but there is space for evidence-informed curation.
- Opportunity: "3 kits by context" framework (morning, school-day, decompression)

### Gap D: Elopement and water safety in summer-to-school transition
Still a high-urgency topic that remains underrepresented in many content calendars.
- Opportunity: emergency plan templates + prevention scripts + school handoff checklist
