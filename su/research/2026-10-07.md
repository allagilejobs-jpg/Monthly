## Fresh Content Angles

1. **"The Halloween Permission Slip"**
   - Carousel giving parents permission to skip costumes, leave early, visit 3 houses, use headphones, bring familiar snacks, or celebrate at home.
   - CTA: "Comment HALLOWEEN and I'll send the sensory checklist."
   - Tool fit: Sensory Profile Quiz.

2. **"Blue Bucket vs Real Inclusion"**
   - Explain that buckets and badges can help, but neighbors should not require eye contact, spoken "trick or treat," costumes, or forced greetings.
   - Make it neighbor-shareable: "Send this to your neighborhood group before Halloween."
   - Strong share value beyond autism-parent audiences.

3. **"AAC Is Not Giving Up On Speech"**
   - Use AAC Awareness Month to answer the fear parents rarely say out loud.
   - Slides: what AAC is, why it can reduce frustration, how to model without pressure, what to ask school/SLP.
   - Tool fit: Beacon for personalized communication questions.

4. **"When Reading Homework Looks Like Defiance"**
   - Tie Dyslexia Awareness Month to autistic children who refuse homework, avoid school, melt down after reading, or seem "fine" until writing demands hit.
   - Reframe: the behavior may be communicating overload.
   - Tool fit: Meltdown Tracker for patterns around school demands.

5. **"World OT Day: What OT Actually Helps With"**
   - Parent-friendly breakdown: sensory regulation, fine motor, feeding, dressing, transitions, classroom participation, and daily routines.
   - Good chance to show OT as practical daily-life support, not vague therapy jargon.

## Notes for SU Voice

- Keep the tone warm and practical: "Here is what you can try tonight."
- Avoid shame-based Halloween language like "make them participate."
- Use parent permission as the emotional hook: participation can mean watching from the porch, visiting one trusted neighbor, or choosing a home movie night.
- Make community-facing Halloween content shareable without sounding scolding.
- Keep tool mentions specific:
  - Sensory Profile Quiz: Halloween costumes, lights, noise, crowds, OT content.
  - Meltdown Tracker: trick-or-treat exits, schoolwork refusal, after-school crashes.
  - Beacon: AAC questions, IEP/504 decisions, school communication support.
