## 4. Fresh Content Angles

### Angle 1: The first week is data

Carousel idea:
1. "The first week is data, not a verdict."
2. Track sleep, appetite, toileting, pickup mood, homework tolerance, and recovery time.
3. Ask the teacher about lunch, hallway, recess, and transitions.
4. Watch for delayed stress, not just school-day behavior.
5. Use patterns to request supports.
6. CTA: "Comment SCHOOL for the first-week tracker."

Why it works: it fits the current week exactly and gives parents a practical action instead of vague reassurance.

### Angle 2: Your child's safety plan should not start after elopement

Carousel idea:
1. "School safety is an accommodation."
2. What elopement can look like.
3. Add transition supports before high-risk times.
4. Ask for a calm response protocol.
5. Include bullying and overwhelm signals.
6. CTA: "Save this for your next teacher email."

Why it works: safety is showing up in competitor content, but SU can make it parent-scripted and school-specific.

### Angle 3: Speech is not the only school participation

Carousel idea for September 23 or October AAC month:
1. "Communication access belongs in every class."
2. AAC should not stay in the backpack.
3. Gestures, signs, visuals, devices, typing, and pointing all count.
4. How to write this into a teacher note or IEP.
5. What to say if staff says, "They did not need it today."
6. CTA: "Comment AAC for the wording."

Why it works: International Day of Sign Languages and AAC Awareness Month are close together, and SU has AAC planned but not yet fully built out.

### Angle 4: When fidgets become trendy, autistic kids still need access

Short-form idea:
- Hook: "Fidgets are mainstream now, but accommodations are not toys."
- Message: If the whole class has squishies, your child's sensory tool still needs a purpose, plan, and protected access.
- Tie to IEP/504: "Access to regulation tools as needed, not earned, removed, or used as a reward."

Why it works: It responds to current back-to-school trends while staying specific to autism parenting.
