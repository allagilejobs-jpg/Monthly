# Spectrum Unlocked Content Research - 2026-09-30

Research run: Wednesday, September 30, 2026, 12:00 PM ET

## Executive Summary

October is the richest upcoming content window for Spectrum Unlocked because multiple neurodiversity, disability, and school-support observances overlap. The best immediate content lane is "October neurodiversity stack": ADHD, dyslexia, AAC, Down syndrome, dysgraphia, cerebral palsy, mental health, stuttering, and occupational therapy. This creates a strong bridge from autism parenting into co-occurring needs, school accommodations, communication supports, and parent advocacy.

## Top Trending Topic

**School choice, IEP rights, and what families lose or keep when moving between public, charter, and private schools.**

Why it matters:
- Understood published a September 10, 2026 article on school choice and special education, emphasizing that public, charter, and private schools do not offer the same guarantees around evaluation, IEPs, services, and accommodations.
- This is highly relevant to autism parents in the fall semester because families are discovering whether supports are actually being delivered.
- It pairs naturally with existing SU Day 10 IEP content but adds a sharper parent decision angle.

Suggested post hooks:
- "Before you switch schools, check what happens to your child's IEP."
- "Public, charter, private: your child's support rights are not the same everywhere."
- "The school may be a better fit, but will the services follow?"

Source: https://www.understood.org/en/articles/school-choice-special-education

## Fresh Content Angles

1. **"October Is Not Just One Awareness Month"**
   - Carousel mapping October observances to autism parenting realities.
   - Slide examples: ADHD and emotional regulation, dyslexia and school refusal, AAC and behavior as communication, OT and sensory overload.
   - CTA: "Which one shows up most in your home?"

2. **"IEP Rights Change by School Type"**
   - Explain public vs charter vs private in parent-friendly language.
   - Angle: choosing a school is also choosing a support structure.
   - CTA: "Save before touring a new school."

3. **"When Reading Problems Look Like Behavior Problems"**
   - Tie Dyslexia Awareness Month to autistic students who melt down, avoid homework, or shut down because decoding and writing are overloaded.
   - Reframe: refusal may be communication, not laziness.

4. **"AAC Awareness for Autism Parents Who Are Nervous About AAC"**
   - Address the common fear that AAC will stop speech.
   - Practical angle: communication support can reduce frustration and behavior intensity.
   - Strong fit for SU's behavior = communication voice.

5. **"World OT Day: What OT Actually Helps With"**
   - Parent-friendly breakdown: sensory regulation, fine motor, feeding, transitions, dressing, school participation.
   - Good bridge into SU Sensory Profile Quiz.
